# Decisions

Choices made and why. Append-only: superseding a decision means adding a new entry that
references the old one and marking the old one **Superseded**. Never edit history.

Kept short by design — read at the start of every session.

---

## D1 — Learn the playbook before committing to a format
**Date:** 2026-08-25 · **Status:** Active

Treat this as a research project first. The deliverable of the research phase is a strategy
document, not a launched channel.

**Why:** Choosing a format before understanding the trade-offs means optimizing inside a
decision that was never examined. The cost of a few weeks of research is small next to
months spent on the wrong medium.

**Would change our mind:** Discovering that the research has sharply diminishing returns and
that the only real way to learn is to publish and observe.

---

## D2 — Keep all four formats in scope for now
**Date:** 2026-08-25 · **Status:** Active

Newsletters, YouTube, podcasts, and short-form social all stay in scope. Narrow later,
driven by the research.

**Why:** Follows from D1. The format choice should be an output of the analysis, not an input.

**Would change our mind:** A format turning out to be clearly disqualified by the 5-10 hr/week
constraint (see D4), in which case drop it early rather than researching it fully.

---

## D3 — Research niches along two tracks, then compare
**Date:** 2026-08-25 · **Status:** Active

Track A anchors on existing domain edge (AI, sales/GTM, B2B software). Track B is an open
market scan on business attractiveness alone. Score both on one shared rubric.

**Why:** Domain edge and market attractiveness are genuinely different bets and the trade-off
between them isn't obvious in advance. Running only one track means never seeing the price of
the choice.

**Would change our mind:** One track producing obviously dominant candidates early, making
further work on the other a poor use of limited time.

---

## D4 — Treat 5-10 hrs/week as a hard constraint
**Date:** 2026-08-25 · **Status:** Active

Apply the capacity budget as a filter throughout the research, not as a final check.

**Why:** Most published creator advice implicitly assumes full-time effort. A strategy that
only works at 30 hrs/week is not a strategy for this project, however good it looks.

**Would change our mind:** A deliberate decision to increase the time commitment.

---

## D5 — Split decisions from learnings; add a phase-synthesis layer
**Date:** 2026-08-25 · **Status:** Active

Four kinds of file: per-resource summaries, phase synthesis in `research/`, durable facts in
`LEARNINGS.md`, and choices here. Structure documented in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md).

**Why:** Decisions and learnings age differently — revisiting a choice shouldn't mean
re-reading a pile of facts. And synthesis across resources needed a home of its own, or it
would have been crammed into the distilled files and made them unskimmable.

**Would change our mind:** The structure adding friction without adding clarity in practice.
