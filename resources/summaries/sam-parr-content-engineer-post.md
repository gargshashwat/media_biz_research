## What can be said from the fragment

**1. "Content engineer" as a self-description, and the horse-salesman line, both point the same way:**
Parr appears to be saying his craft is being made obsolete — that the way he makes content (intuition,
iteration, "making it up as I go") is the horse trade at the moment the car arrives. **Given the
Rowan Cheung material studied alongside it — AI avatars, Claude projects trained on your own voice,
90%-ready drafts — this reads as the same observation from the pessimist's side.**

**2. The stated method is post → react → iterate**, which matches Imran's account almost exactly
("I was just posting random stuff... oh, that seemed to work, let's double down"). **Two operators
with large audiences describing method as emergent rather than planned.**
