## What's useful for this project

**1. ⚠️ The 50/50 split between social content and the newsletter itself is the most useful thing
here, and it's corroborated.** Poyar said the same thing independently and more forcefully:

> "You're writing and 80% of your time is on the content... **you've got to treat the audience and
> the content on essentially equal footing.**"

**Two sources now say roughly half your production time goes to distribution, not to the product.**
For a 5-10 hr/week budget that is a hard planning constraint: **it effectively halves the writing
time available**, and any plan that assumes otherwise is wrong before it starts.

**2. Design as differentiation is now a three-source theme.** Poyar called infographics "the secret
behind my growth" and builds them in Google Slides from a fixed template. Fuller rebuilt Flying
Magazine around being "coffee table ready." Alder says the same. **Notably, none of them use a
designer for the routine work** — Poyar explicitly argues that over-polished work reads as corporate
marketing and gets skipped.

**3. A concrete, cheap tool stack** — Canva, Figma, Screen Studio, Xnapper — none requiring design
skill or hiring. Worth keeping as a starting point rather than researching tooling from scratch later.
