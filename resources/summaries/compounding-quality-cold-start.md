## What it claims

An asset manager with no audience started posting on Twitter, had **4 followers after a week**, and
reached hundreds of thousands of followers and a six-figure subscriber list within about two years —
on volume, repurposing, and cross-platform loops rather than on any single breakout credential.

## Specific numbers and facts

- **Background:** Belgian, **~3 years as an asset manager** before going full-time on the newsletter
  in **July 2022**.
- **The actual cold start: at least 20 tweets per day.** After **one week he had 4 followers** —
  reportedly including his brother and his best friend.
- **Then a discontinuity:** one night the count jumped from a handful to **~1,500**, which he
  describes as the point the snowball started. ⚠️ *Nothing in the accessible material explains what
  caused the jump — this is the single most important unexplained step in the case.*
- **100,000 newsletter subscribers in 14 months** (Growth In Reverse's framing).
- **0 → 440,000+ followers in ~2 years** across platforms. Component figures given elsewhere:
  ~430,000 on X and ~210,000 on LinkedIn (⚠️ these do not reconcile with the 440,000 total — see
  caveats). Newsletter ~350,000 readers; **>1M readers across all platforms.**

### The three named tactics

1. **Cross-platform growth loops.** The Tuesday email carried five bullets, **at least one of which
   pointed to CQ content on X or LinkedIn** — so the owned list was used to push readers *back* to
   the rented platforms, amplifying both. Most operators only run this loop in one direction.
2. **Aggressive repurposing.** Rather than always writing new material, he **reposted the same piece
   repeatedly with improved captions** — one post ("the 10 greatest acquisitions of all time") was
   run **five separate times**.
3. **Lead magnets.** Compilations of expert content given away in exchange for an email address —
   converting rented attention into the owned list by default.

## What's useful for this project

**1. It is a real answer to "how do you start from zero followers."** The answer here is
uncomfortable but clear: **very high volume, sustained, before anything works at all.** Twenty posts
a day for a week produced four followers. That is the honest shape of the first month.

**2. The reverse growth loop is a genuinely new mechanic for this project.** Every other case treats
the owned list as the terminal destination ([L7](../../LEARNINGS.md)). Slegers pushes list → social
as well as social → list. Cheap to run and directly actionable.

**3. Repurposing is the only tactic found that materially cuts the distribution cost.** Reposting one
winner five times is the clearest lever against [L15](../../LEARNINGS.md) — half of production time
going to distribution — at a 5-10 hr/week budget.

**4. ⚠️ It contradicts the "one epic piece" mechanic, and the contradiction is informative.** Lenny's
growth came from two enormous research projects; Slegers' came from daily volume of relatively short,
repeatable posts. **The difference tracks the audience:** a broad retail readership rewards frequency
and accessibility; a professional readership rewards depth. See
[07-early-stage-marketing.md](../../research/07-early-stage-marketing.md).

## Caveats

- **⚠️ No primary source was readable** (egress policy blocked growthinreverse.com,
  good-investing.net and compoundingquality.net). Everything here is second-hand.
- **⚠️ The follower figures don't reconcile.** "440,000 followers in 2 years" against "~430,000 on X
  and ~210,000 on LinkedIn" cannot both be right as stated. Treat the *shape* as the finding and the
  numbers as unreliable.
- **⚠️ The audience is the wrong persona for [D1](../../DECISIONS.md).** This is a large, broad,
  largely **retail** investing audience — the [L1](../../LEARNINGS.md) low-revenue-per-reader side
  of the table, not Citrini's $999 professionals. **Tactics optimized for retail reach should not be
  assumed to transfer to a professional-investor publication**, and some actively work against it.
- **⚠️ Full-time from July 2022**, with three years of asset-management credibility behind him. Not
  a nights-and-weekends case, and another instance of [L14](../../LEARNINGS.md).
- **The unexplained overnight jump from ~4 to ~1,500 followers is the crux and it is undocumented.**
  Without knowing what caused it, the case cannot be treated as a repeatable playbook.
